Avatar V4 — Ryan SSJ1, SSJ2, SSJ3

A clean PixelLab rebuild. One canonical Ryan identity was generated first, then each form was made as a character state of that same identity — never chained off another form, so a defect in one cannot travel into the next. Static artwork only: no transformation animation, no atlases, nothing touched under ios/.

The identity is deliberately built with a close-cropped dark scalp. That is structural, not a design choice: it means each form receives exactly one hairstyle, so there is no second hairstyle underneath to show through at the roots. The audit treats that scalp's own shades as forbidden inside every transformed form — if a gold hairstyle failed to cover the crown, the base's dark hair would read as roots just as the old brown curls did.

Why each rejection

  • call 1 — invented grey rectangular patch on the chest of the black shirt (approx 12px, visible in south / south-east / south-west). The brief forbids logos, badges and invented clothing marks, and this base propagates to all three forms.
  • call 2 — white/light stripes down both sleeves and both pant legs, plus orange shoe accents — invented trim, which the brief forbids (plain unmarked black shirt and pants)
  • call 4 — superseded by the wide-stance alternate 3fe10f3a — this one is otherwise clean but its stance is narrower than reference frame 3 requires
